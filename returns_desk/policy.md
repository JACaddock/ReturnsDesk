# Larkfield Goods returns and refunds policy

## 1. About this policy

### 1.1 Who this policy covers

This policy applies to everything bought from the Larkfield Goods website by customers in the United Kingdom.
It doesn't affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Where this policy and the law
differ, whichever gives you more protection applies.

### 1.2 Confirming who you are

Before we can discuss an order, we need to confirm that it's yours. Please give us the order number, which
starts with the letter A and appears in your confirmation email, and the email address you used to place the
order. We can't share order details, start a return or issue a refund until both match our records. After
three unsuccessful attempts we will pass your enquiry to a member of the team for security reasons.

## 2. Returning an item

### 2.1 The return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the date they were delivered, for any reason. The 30 days start on
the delivery date shown in your order, not the date you placed the order. If your order arrived in more than
one parcel, each item's window starts on the day that item was delivered.

### 2.2 The condition items must be in

Items must be unused and returned with all their parts, cables, manuals and accessories. Original packaging is
helpful but not required. If an item comes back damaged, used or with parts missing, we may reduce the refund
to reflect its condition, or return it to you.

### 2.3 How to start a return

Contact customer support with your order number and email address, and tell us which item you'd like to
return and why. We'll confirm the details with you before anything is processed. Once the return has been
started, you'll receive a prepaid returns label by email. The item must be sent back within 14 days of the
return being started.

### 2.4 Returning part of an order

You can return individual items from an order that contained several. Each item is treated separately, and any
refund only covers the items you send back.

### 2.5 Gifts

If you received an item as a gift, the person who bought it needs to start the return, because we can only
discuss an order with the customer who placed it. The refund will go to their original payment method.

## 3. Items that can't be returned

### 3.1 Final-sale and clearance items

Items marked as final sale, including everything bought from the clearance range, can't be returned or
exchanged unless they are faulty. This is shown on the product page and in your order confirmation.

### 3.2 Headphones and earbuds

For hygiene reasons, headphones and earbuds can't be returned once they have been opened, unwrapped or worn,
unless they are faulty. Unopened headphones and earbuds in their sealed packaging can be returned as normal
within 30 days.

### 3.3 Personalised items

Items that have been engraved or personalised to your request can't be returned unless they are faulty or the
personalisation is wrong because of our mistake.

## 4. Faulty or damaged items

### 4.1 Items that arrive damaged

If an item arrives broken, damaged or with parts missing, please tell us as soon as you can, ideally within
48 hours of delivery. Within 30 days of delivery you can choose a full refund or a replacement, and return
postage is free.

### 4.2 Faults that develop later

If an item develops a fault within 12 months of delivery, you're entitled to a repair or a replacement. If a
repair or replacement isn't possible, or doesn't fix the problem, you can have a refund. This applies to
final-sale, opened and clearance items too.

### 4.3 Faults reported after 30 days

For faults reported more than 30 days after delivery, a member of the team will review the request before a
refund, repair or replacement is agreed. We may ask you to send the item back for inspection first.

### 4.4 Minor cosmetic damage

If an item arrives with a small scratch, dent or mark but works properly, you can keep it and accept a partial
refund instead of returning it. The amount depends on the damage, and the usual approval limit in section 5.2
applies.

### 4.5 Evidence

To help us resolve things quickly, we may ask for a photo of the damage or the fault. We'll never ask you to
pay to send a faulty item back.

## 5. Refunds

### 5.1 How and when refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 working days of being approved. For returned
items, the refund is approved once the item has arrived back at our warehouse and been checked, which is
usually within 3 working days of it reaching us. We can't refund to a different card or account.

### 5.2 Approval limits

Customer support can approve refunds of up to £75 in total per order straight away, after confirming the
amount with you. Larger refunds, or a further refund that would take the total for an order over £75, are
reviewed by a member of the team, who will contact you within 2 working days.

### 5.3 The most we can refund

A refund can never be more than the amount paid for the order, including any earlier refunds on the same order.

### 5.4 Original delivery charges

If you return a whole order within 30 days, we refund the original delivery charge as well. If you return only
part of an order, the delivery charge isn't refunded, unless the item was faulty or sent in error.

## 6. Return postage

### 6.1 Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

Returning a faulty, damaged or incorrect item is free. We'll email a prepaid label.

### 6.2 Other returns

For returns that aren't because of a fault or our mistake, £3.95 is deducted from the refund to cover postage.
The prepaid label is still provided.

### 6.3 Dropping off a return

Parcels can be dropped off at any Post Office or parcel locker that accepts Royal Mail. Keep your proof of
postage until the refund has been processed.

## 7. Delivery and missing orders

### 7.1 Delivery times

Orders are usually dispatched within 2 working days and delivered within 1 to 4 working days after dispatch.
You'll get an email with the carrier and tracking number once your order has been dispatched.

### 7.2 Tracking an order

You can track a dispatched order on the carrier's website using the tracking number in your dispatch email.
Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch.

### 7.3 Parcels that haven't arrived

If a parcel hasn't arrived 7 working days after it was dispatched, contact us and we will investigate with the
carrier. If the carrier can't find it, we will send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

### 7.4 Parcels marked as delivered but not received

If tracking says your parcel was delivered but you haven't received it, please check with neighbours and any
safe place, then contact us within 7 days. A member of the team will investigate with the carrier.

### 7.5 Changing the delivery address

We can't change the delivery address once an order has been placed. If the address is wrong, contact us
straight away and we'll cancel the order if it's still processing.

## 8. Cancelling an order

### 8.1 Before dispatch

An order can be cancelled for free while its status is "processing". You'll receive a full refund within 5 to
10 working days.

### 8.2 After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched it can't be cancelled, but it can be returned under section 2 after it
arrives.

## 9. Prices and promotions

### 9.1 Price changes

We don't price-match other retailers, and we don't refund the difference if the price of an item goes down
after you've bought it.

### 9.2 Discount codes

Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes can't be added after an order has been placed. If you
return an item bought with a discount, the refund is the amount you actually paid for it.

## 10. Speaking to a person

### 10.1 Our team

You can ask to speak to a member of the team at any time. They work Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and will reply
within 2 working days.

### 10.2 Complaints

If you're unhappy with how your enquiry was handled, ask for it to be passed to the team as a complaint. A
complaint is acknowledged within 2 working days and answered in full within 10 working days.
